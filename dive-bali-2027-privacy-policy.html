Privacy Policy

Amazing Soulcations · Effective July 2026
This is the master Privacy Policy for Amazing Soulcations and applies to every trip we host, regardless of which host or partner brand is running it. It does not change per trip.

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. By submitting your information through any of our registration forms, inquiry forms, or email capture, you agree to the terms of this policy.

What Information We Collect

When you express interest in a trip hosted by Amazing Soulcations, we may collect the following:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your room preference (double or single occupancy)
  • Any additional information you choose to share in a message field

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the purpose of planning and administering the trip you've expressed interest in or registered for. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Follow up on your interest and answer your questions
  • Send you trip-related information, updates, and materials
  • Confirm and manage your reservation
  • Coordinate logistics for the trip

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes. Your information is used only in connection with the trip you've registered for.

Who Has Access to Your Information

Your information is accessible only to the host and administrator of your specific trip, and to Amazing Soulcations for record-keeping and support purposes. It may be shared with third parties strictly as required to fulfill trip logistics, including but not limited to:

  • The hotel or resort, for check-in and room assignments
  • Local activity providers, dive operators, and excursion companies that require guest names or waivers
  • Transportation providers for transfers and local travel

Your information will never be shared with any of these parties for marketing or promotional purposes, only as necessary to deliver the services included in your trip.

How We Store Your Information

Your information is stored securely in our email and administrative systems. We take reasonable precautions to protect it from unauthorized access. We retain your information for the duration of the trip and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping purposes.

Your Rights

You may request to review, update, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting your trip administrator or Amazing Soulcations directly. If you no longer wish to receive communications from us, simply let us know and we will remove you promptly.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, please contact your trip administrator or reach out to Amazing Soulcations directly.

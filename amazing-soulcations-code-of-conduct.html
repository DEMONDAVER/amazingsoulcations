Code of Conduct

Amazing Soulcations · Effective July 2026
This is the master Code of Conduct for Amazing Soulcations and applies to every trip we host, regardless of host or destination. It does not change per trip.

Amazing Soulcations trips are built around small groups and genuine connection, both with fellow travelers and with the places and people we visit. This Code of Conduct exists to protect that experience for everyone on the trip.

Expectations of Every Guest

All guests are expected to treat fellow travelers, local staff, guides, and community members with courtesy and respect throughout the trip. This includes being mindful of local customs, dress codes, and cultural norms at each destination.

What Will Not Be Tolerated

  • Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior toward any guest, staff member, or local community member
  • Illegal activity of any kind, including drug use
  • Behavior that endangers the safety of yourself, others, or the environment
  • Repeated disregard for group schedules, safety briefings, or the reasonable instructions of guides and staff

Consequences

The Organizer reserves the right to remove any guest from the trip, without refund, for violation of this Code of Conduct. A removed guest is solely responsible for their own return travel arrangements and any associated costs. Minor issues will typically be addressed directly and privately by the host before any more serious action is considered.

Alcohol & Substance Use

Where alcohol is available on a trip, guests are expected to drink responsibly. The Organizer reserves the right to refuse service or remove any guest whose behavior while under the influence becomes disruptive, unsafe, or disrespectful to other guests or staff.

Group Culture

Our trips are intentionally small so that every guest gets a personal experience. We ask every guest to help protect that: be on time for group activities, be considerate of shared spaces, and extend the same welcome to fellow travelers that you'd want extended to you.
